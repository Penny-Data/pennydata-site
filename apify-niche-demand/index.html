How to read it

  • High users per actor and a small leader share is the friendly case. JOBS (618 users per actor, leader holds 25%) fits; the demand is spread over many actors.
  • High users per actor and a big leader share means one actor is the niche. TRAVEL looks rich at 1,318 users per actor, but compass/crawler-google-places alone has 70.8% of its users. SEO_TOOLS (54.7%) and NEWS (50.5%) are similar.
  • Large categories with low users per actor are crowded. AUTOMATION has 397 actors in the file at 468 each, and the biggest holds only 8.1%; LEAD_GENERATION and SOCIAL_MEDIA are the largest by total users, with 491 and 479 actors splitting them.

Limits of this data

The file holds only the top 1,000 actors by 30-day users out of roughly 100,000 in the Store, so it shows where established demand is, not the long tail. Categories are the Store's own, which are broad; a real niche (for example "Google Maps reviews" or "Greenhouse jobs") sits inside one. The numbers are a point-in-time snapshot.
