Hacker News "Who is hiring?": search the latest thread

The monthly thread has hundreds of job posts and no search box. Load the newest one, filter by keyword (python, remote, Berlin, Rust) and open the posts that match.

How it works

Your browser downloads the thread from the public Hacker News Algolia API, strips the HTML and filters the posts locally, so you can change keywords without reloading. The table shows the first 100 matches; the CSV has them all with the full text. Free use is limited to 10 thread loads per hour per browser.

Want every post as clean rows (company, role, location, remote, salary, links) across months? The bulk version parses the thread into structured data: HN Who Is Hiring Parser on Apify.

Privacy: no analytics, no cookies; the free-use counter stays in your browser's local storage. The thread is fetched from hn.algolia.com; your keywords never leave your browser.
