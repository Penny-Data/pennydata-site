Domain WHOIS and DNS lookup

Paste up to 10 domains, one per line. You get the registration, expiry and last-changed dates and the registrar from RDAP (the modern replacement for WHOIS), plus the domain's A, MX, NS and TXT records.

How it works

Your browser asks Google Public DNS (dns.google, DNS-over-HTTPS) for the records and rdap.org for the registration data. rdap.org forwards the query to the registry for that domain's ending. Some country-code endings (for example .de or .co.uk) do not publish RDAP, so their dates show as "not published". Free use is limited to 50 domains per hour per browser.

Need hundreds or thousands of domains? The bulk version checks up to 1,000 domains per run (registrar, expiry, domain age, nameservers, plus an MX/SPF/DMARC mode) and exports CSV or JSON: Bulk Domain WHOIS and DNS Lookup on Apify.

Privacy: no analytics, no cookies; the free-use counter stays in your browser's local storage. The domains you enter are sent from your browser to dns.google and rdap.org only.
