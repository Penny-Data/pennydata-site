How it works

Your browser asks Polymarket's public Gamma API directly (search results, current prices); nothing goes through our servers. Prices are a snapshot of what the page loaded, not trading advice. Free use is limited to 60 searches per hour per browser.

Need odds for hundreds of markets, on a schedule? The bulk version exports markets with prices, volume, liquidity and end dates, filtered by topic or tag: Polymarket Markets Scraper on Apify.

Privacy: no analytics, no cookies; the free-use counter stays in your browser's local storage. Your search term is sent from your browser to gamma-api.polymarket.com.
