How the free use works

The WHOIS/DNS, image, Polymarket and Hacker News tools run entirely in your browser. The contact finder runs on a free Hugging Face Space. The browser tools have a small per-browser hourly limit so the free services they call stay usable for everyone; the contact finder follows Hugging Face's own fair-use limits.

Privacy: this site has no analytics, no cookies and no account. The hourly free-use counter is kept only in your browser's local storage. Domains you look up go straight from your browser to public DNS (dns.google) and registry RDAP services (rdap.org). Pasted HTML never leaves your browser. The contact finder page embeds a Hugging Face Space, which receives the domain you enter.
