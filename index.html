How the free use works

The WHOIS/DNS and image tools run entirely in your browser. The contact finder runs on a free Hugging Face Space. Each tool has a small per-browser limit so the free services it calls stay usable for everyone.

Privacy: this site has no analytics, no cookies and no account. Domains you look up go straight from your browser to public DNS (dns.google) and registry RDAP services (rdap.org). Pasted HTML never leaves your browser. The contact finder page embeds a Hugging Face Space, which receives the domain you enter.
